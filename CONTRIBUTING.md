## Who Can Contribute

Contributions are accepted **only** from:

- Space agencies — ISRO, NASA, ESA
- Accredited research institutions
- Aerospace industry partners — with written agreement
- Individuals with expertise domain knowledge & explicit written permission
  from Gouragopal Mohapatra

---

## How to Request Collaboration

If you are a researcher, space agency representative,
or aerospace professional interested in collaborating:

1. Open a GitHub Issue titled:
   `[Collaboration Request] — Your Name / Organisation`

2. Include:
   - Your name and affiliation
   - Nature of proposed collaboration
   - How it aligns with AstroVital's mission

3. All collaboration is subject to written agreement.

---

## Bug Reports

If you find a technical issue:

1. Open a GitHub Issue
2. Title: `[Bug] — Brief description`
3. Include notebook name, block number, error message

- Modifications to core innovations
- Attempts to reproduce or redistribute this work
- Commercial use requests
