## 1. Governance Philosophy

The ASTROVITAL AI program operates under a single-authority research governance model. All research direction, architectural decisions, version release approvals, contribution evaluations, intellectual property determinations, and public communications originate from and are authorized by the program's sole researcher, founder, and intellectual property owner — **Gouragopal Mohapatra**.

This governance structure is not a limitation — it is a deliberate and appropriate design for an independent research program in its foundational phase. It ensures clarity of vision, consistency of research direction, unambiguous IP attribution, and accountability for all technical and scientific claims made under the AstroVital AI brand.

As the program matures through its multi-version roadmap and potentially expands to include collaborators, institutional partners, or organizational stakeholders, this governance document will be updated to reflect the expanded decision-making structure. All such updates will be versioned, dated, and committed to the public repository record.

### 3.3 — Version Completion Criteria

A version is considered complete and eligible for release authorization when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. All defined innovations for the version are implemented, tested, and documented
2. All performance benchmarks defined in the research plan are evaluated and reported
3. All Jupyter Notebooks are committed, reviewed, and output-cleared for public release
4. All model artifacts are serialized, verified, and checksummed
5. All documentation — README, technical reports, and release notes — are finalized
6. The `CHANGELOG.md` entry for the version is complete
7. Explicit release authorization is granted by the program founder

## 5. Research Integrity Policy

### 5.1 — Standards

All research conducted under the ASTROVITAL AI program adheres to the following standards of scientific integrity:

- **Reproducibility:** All results are reproducible from the publicly committed notebooks and datasets. No result is reported that cannot be independently verified from the repository record.
- **Honest Limitation Disclosure:** All known limitations of the system — including synthetic dataset constraints, precision-recall tradeoffs, and scope boundaries — are documented openly in the project README and technical reports.
- **Transparent Methodology:** All algorithmic choices, hyperparameter configurations, threshold decisions, and evaluation methodologies are documented in full within the project notebooks.
- **No Fabrication or Falsification:** No result, metric, or claim in any project document has been fabricated, selectively reported, or misrepresented. All performance figures are derived from the actual model evaluation outputs committed to the repository.

### 5.2 — Replication and Audit

Any researcher who wishes to independently replicate the results of ASTROVITAL AI — VITALX CORE V1 may do so by cloning the repository, installing the requirements specified in `requirements.txt`, and executing the notebooks in order within the `VITALX_LAB/` directory. The complete pipeline, from raw data generation to final Artemis II simulation output, is reproducible without modification.

Discrepancies identified during independent replication should be reported as GitHub Issues with the label `[Replication Issue]` and will be investigated and addressed by the program founder.
